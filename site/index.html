Graph browser for the classified Common Reference Model, diseases, procedures and drugs. There is no live reasoner: the hierarchy is the one the GALEN team published in 2010, with property chains.

The terminology and the derived data are under the GALEN Open Source License. Unmodified sources are in sources/. The browser code is MIT. Repository.

Controls

  • Generated, Siblings, Labels, and Clustering are switches beside the search box. Clustering is on at each start. Off shows every ancestor as its own node.
  • A dashed +n pill is a cluster of intermediate ancestors. Click the pill to expand it. Click the coloured background of an expanded cluster, not a node, to collapse that cluster again.
  • The zoom tools sit on the graph: zoom in, zoom out, 1:1 (layout scale), and fit-all. Drag the background to pan. The wheel pans. Ctrl or ⌘ and the wheel zoom.
  • The side panel slides off to the right. The narrow strip keeps two icon buttons: show the concept again, and open this panel. The strip remembers whether it was open.
  • Hover a node for its name without changing focus. Hover a collapsed cluster to read the hidden names. The side panel is the focused concept: definition, sanctions, rubric, parents, and children.
  • Click a concept to refocus it. The address is #concept=Name.
